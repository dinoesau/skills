# Paper Craft Examples

## 1. Research article skeleton

1. Title with phenomenon, variables, design.
2. Structured abstract: objective, methods with sample, key result with magnitude, takeaway.
3. Introduction funnel ending in one hypothesis.
4. Methods with design, participants, materials, procedures, analysis, ethics.
5. Results in aim order with one table and one figure referenced by number.
6. Discussion linking each claim to a result plus limitations.
7. Conclusion answering the question in two paragraphs.

## 2. Philosophy essay skeleton

1. Thesis: "I argue that X because R1 and R2."
2. Definitions of X, R1, R2.
3. Argument 1 in P1/P2/C form with premise defense.
4. Argument 2 in P1/P2/C form with premise defense.
5. Strongest objection 1 stated fairly plus reply.
6. Strongest objection 2 stated fairly plus reply or thesis narrowing.
7. Conclusion: refined thesis and what the literature should take from it.

## 3. Opinion op-ed skeleton, 700 words

1. Hook: one concrete scene or surprising number.
2. Thesis in one sentence plus why now.
3. Three supporting moves, one paragraph each, each with one fact or example.
4. Best counter in two sentences plus one-sentence answer.
5. Close: what the reader should do or believe differently from tomorrow.
