# Prose Examples

## 1. Bloated to concise, claim preserved

Before: "It is absolutely certain in our opinion that the future implementation of the new caching process will without any doubt lead to very significant improvements in terms of overall system performance in most cases."

After: "The new cache appears to improve median response time; staging shows a 20 percent drop across three runs."

Changes: Concision cut pleonasms and nominalizations. Precision added hedge plus measured value. Claim strength unchanged.

## 2. Emotive to neutral academic tone

Before: "This marvelous result demolishes the rival theory, which is completely unsustainable and frankly embarrassing."

After: "This result favors our hypothesis over the rival account, which predicts the opposite pattern and does not fit the observed data."

Changes: Voice replaced value adjectives with checkable contrast. Precision tied the verdict to a prediction mismatch.

## 3. Disconnected to cohesive

Before: "Moreover, the sample is small. Moreover, the method is new. Moreover, replication is pending."

After: "The sample is small. The method is also new, so replication is pending before generalizing."

Changes: Cohesion rotated connectors by function. Rhythm merged fragments into one flowing sentence with a causal link.
